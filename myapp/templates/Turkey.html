Travel Information

Turkey has well-developed transport systems including metro lines in Istanbul, Ankara, and Izmir. The country has major international airports such as Istanbul Airport and Sabiha Gökçen Airport. Domestic flights, high-speed YHT trains, intercity buses, and ferries are widely available.
