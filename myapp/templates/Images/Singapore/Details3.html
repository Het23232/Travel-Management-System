The Fullerton Hotel

**The Fullerton Hotel Singapore** is a **historic and luxurious waterfront hotel**, blending **heritage, elegance, and world-class service**. Originally the **General Post Office building**, this hotel offers a stunning blend of colonial charm and modern sophistication.

  • Elegant Colonial Architecture
  • Luxurious Suites with Personalized Services
  • Award-Winning Dining Experiences
  • Infinity Pool Overlooking Marina Bay
  • Fullerton Spa & Wellness Retreat
