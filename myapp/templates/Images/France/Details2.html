Le Meurice, Paris

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (4.9/5)

**Le Meurice** is a historic **5-star palace hotel** in **Paris**, known for its **artistic grandeur, Michelin-star dining, and opulent suites**. Overlooking the **Tuileries Garden**, the hotel embodies the essence of French elegance and refinement.

Hotel Features & Amenities

  • Lavish Rooms & Suites with Artistic Interiors
  • Michelin-Starred Restaurant by Alain Ducasse
  • World-Class Spa & Wellness Facilities
  • Exclusive Afternoon Tea at Le Dali
  • Panoramic Views of the Tuileries Garden
  • 24/7 Personalized Concierge & Butler Service
  • Prime Location in the Heart of Paris

Location

Address: 228 Rue de Rivoli, 75001 Paris, France
