Hotel Alfonso XIII, Seville

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (4.9/5)

**Hotel Alfonso XIII is a historic five-star luxury hotel located in **Seville, Spain**, commissioned by **King Alfonso XIII** to host international guests. This stunning architectural gem blends **Moorish, Andalusian, and Castilian styles**, offering a unique stay filled with **rich history, lavish interiors, and world-class hospitality**.

Hotel Features & Amenities

  • Historic Landmark Hotel Since 1929
  • Elegant Andalusian Architecture
  • Luxurious Suites with Traditional Decor
  • Fine Dining at Restaurante San Fernando
  • Outdoor Pool & Exclusive Courtyard
  • Located in the Heart of Seville

Location

Address: Calle San Fernando, 2, 41004 Sevilla, Spain
