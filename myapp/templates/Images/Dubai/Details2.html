Atlantis The Palm, Dubai

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (4.9/5)

**Atlantis The Palm** is one of **Dubai’s most iconic luxury resorts**, offering a unique experience on the world-famous **Palm Jumeirah**. With **underwater suites, thrilling waterparks, world-class dining, and a private beach**, this resort is a perfect destination for adventure and relaxation.

Hotel Features & Amenities

  • Underwater Suites & Ocean View Rooms
  • Aquaventure Waterpark – Largest in the World
  • Fine Dining with Michelin-Starred Restaurants
  • The Lost Chambers Aquarium & Dolphin Bay
  • Private Beach & Exclusive Poolside Cabanas
  • Luxury Spa & Fitness Center
  • World-Class Entertainment & Nightlife

Location

Address: Crescent Rd, The Palm Jumeirah, Dubai, United Arab Emirates
