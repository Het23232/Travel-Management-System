Gritti Palace, Venice

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (4.8/5)

The **Gritti Palace** is a luxurious **historic hotel** on the **Grand Canal in Venice**, offering **unparalleled views of the water** and the **Santa Maria della Salute Basilica**. With **exquisite Venetian decor**, **Michelin-star dining**, and a **world-class spa**, it is a true **symbol of elegance and prestige**.

Hotel Features & Amenities

  • Prime Location on the Grand Canal
  • Opulent Venetian Interiors
  • Michelin-Star Dining & Fine Wines
  • World-Class Acqua di Parma Spa
  • Exclusive Terrace with Canal Views
  • Historic 16th-Century Architecture
