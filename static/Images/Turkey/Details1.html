Ciragan Palace Kempinskil

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (4.8/5)

Ciragan Palace Kempinski is a magnificent 19th-century Ottoman palace turned luxury hotel, located on the banks of the Bosphorus in Istanbul, Turkey. It blends the charm of royal heritage with five-star modern elegance. Guests enjoy a truly regal experience with breathtaking views, world-class service, and iconic architecture.

Hotel Features & Amenities

  • Historic Ottoman Palace
  • Bosphorus Waterfront Location
  • Luxurious Palace Suites & Modern Rooms
  • Fine Dining at Tuğra Restaurant
  • Infinity Pool with Bosphorus Views
  • Spa, Turkish Hammam, and Wellness Center

Location

Address: Çırağan Caddesi No:32, Beşiktaş 34349, Istanbul, Turkey
