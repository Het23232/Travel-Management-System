Shangri-La Guangzhou

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (4.7/5)

Surrounded by 58,000 square meters of lush landscaped gardens, Shangri-La Guangzhou serves as a tranquil urban resort alongside the scenic Pearl River. Situated steps from the Canton Fair Complex, the hotel features panoramic riverfront views, award-winning Cantonese gastronomy at Summer Palace, signature treatments at CHI, The Spa, and comprehensive resort facilities.

Hotel Features & Amenities

  • Spacious River-View and Garden-View Rooms & Suites
  • Authentic Cantonese Specialities at Summer Palace & Global Buffets at WOK**121
  • Holistic Treatments and Wellness Journeys at CHI, The Spa
  • Indoor Heated Pool & Outdoor Freeform Lagoon Pool
  • 58,000 m² Landscaped Tropical Gardens with Walking Trails
  • Comprehensive Health Club, Tennis Courts, and Jacuzzi
  • Immediate Access to the Canton Fair Complex (Pazhou Exhibition Center)

Location

Address: 1 Hui Zhan Dong Road, Hai Zhu District, Guangzhou, Guangdong, China
