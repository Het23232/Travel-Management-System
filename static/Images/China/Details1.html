The Peninsula Beijing

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (4.8/5)

The Peninsula Beijing combines traditional Chinese architectural artistry with modern luxury in the heart of Beijing's premier shopping district, Wangfujing. Renowned as an all-suite hotel, it features lavish accommodations, Michelin-starred Cantonese dining at Jing and Huang Ting, bespoke guest experiences, and the celebrated Peninsula Spa.

Hotel Features & Amenities

  • All-Suite Accommodations with Integrated In-Room Smart Technology
  • Michelin-Starred Cantonese Dining at Huang Ting & French Gastronomy at Jing
  • Signature Peninsula Afternoon Tea in the Grand Lobby
  • Holistic Wellness at The Peninsula Spa and Fitness Centre
  • Heated Indoor Swimming Pool
  • Bespoke Concierge Services & Peninsula Fleet Transport
  • Prime Walking Distance to Wangfujing and the Forbidden City

Location

Address: 8 Goldfish Lane, Wangfujing, Dongcheng District, Beijing, China
