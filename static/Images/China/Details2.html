Waldorf Astoria Shanghai on the Bund

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (4.8/5)

Set along the historic waterfront, Waldorf Astoria Shanghai on the Bund blends heritage architecture with contemporary sophistication. Comprising the neoclassical 1910 Waldorf Astoria Club building and a modern high-rise tower, the hotel offers sweeping vistas of the Huangpu River and Pudong skyline, fine culinary experiences, and the legendary Long Bar.

Hotel Features & Amenities

  • Heritage Suites in the Historic Bund Club & Modern Tower Rooms
  • Iconic 110-Foot Long Bar with Waterfront Views & Classic Cocktails
  • Exquisite Dining at Pelham's French Restaurant & Wei Jing Ge Cantonese Cuisine
  • Signature Afternoon Tea at Salon de Ville
  • Waldorf Astoria Spa with Bespoke Treatments
  • Indoor Heated Pool & Fully Equipped Fitness Center
  • Prime Bund Promenade Location near Nanjing Road Pedestrian Street

Location

Address: 2 Zhongshan East 1st Road, Huangpu District, Shanghai, China
