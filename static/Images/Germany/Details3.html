The Westin Hamburg

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (4.6/5)

Housed within the iconic glass wave structure of the Elbphilharmonie concert hall, The Westin Hamburg offers an architectural luxury experience in the heart of the Speicherstadt UNESCO World Heritage district. Featuring floor-to-ceiling panoramic views of the Elbe River and the Port of Hamburg, the hotel pairs modern Scandinavian-inspired design with signature Heavenly wellness amenities.

Hotel Features & Amenities

  • Contemporary Rooms & Suites with Floor-to-Ceiling River & Harbor Views
  • Direct Access to the World-Famous Elbphilharmonie Concert Halls
  • Fine Regional and International Dining at Fang & Feld Restaurant
  • Panoramic Views and Craft Cocktails at The Bridge Bar
  • 1,300 m² Elb Spa Featuring a 20-Meter Heated Indoor Pool and Saunas
  • WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio with Modern Cardio Equipment
  • Prime Waterfront Location in the HafenCity Quarter

Location
