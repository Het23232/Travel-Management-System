Hotel Adlon Kempinski Berlin

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (4.8/5)

Positioned directly beside the historic Brandenburg Gate, Hotel Adlon Kempinski Berlin stands as Germany’s most legendary luxury grand hotel. Combining European grandeur with bespoke service, the hotel features world-class accommodations, two-Michelin-starred fine dining at Lorenz Adlon Esszimmer, and rejuvenating wellness journeys at the Adlon Spa by Resense.

Hotel Features & Amenities

  • Opulent Rooms & Presidential Suites with Direct Brandenburg Gate Views
  • Two-Michelin-Starred Fine Dining at Lorenz Adlon Esszimmer
  • Classic Cocktails and Live Music at the Iconic Lobby Lounge & Bar
  • Adlon Spa by Resense Offering Holistic Luxury Wellness Treatments
  • Heated Indoor Neoclassical Pool, Whirlpool & Fitness Suite
  • 24/7 Dedicated Butler Service and Private Concierge
  • Unrivaled Location on Pariser Platz and Unter den Linden
