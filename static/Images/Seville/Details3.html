Hotel Casa 1800 Sevilla

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (4.8/5)

**Hotel Casa 1800 Sevilla** is a beautifully restored **19th-century mansion** offering a **charming and elegant stay** in the heart of **Seville, Spain**. With its **classic Andalusian courtyards, rooftop pool with stunning city views, and luxurious rooms**, it provides an unforgettable experience for travelers seeking **history, luxury, and tranquility**.

Hotel Features & Amenities

  • Prime Location Near Seville Cathedral
  • Rooftop Terrace with Stunning City Views
  • Elegant and Historic Andalusian Architecture
  • Luxurious Rooms with Traditional Decor
  • Complimentary Afternoon Tea Service
  • Outdoor Pool and Sun Terrace

Address: Calle Rodrigo Caro, 6, 41004 Sevilla, Spain
