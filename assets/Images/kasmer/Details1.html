The Lalit Grand Palace

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (4.8/5)

The **Lalit Grand Palace**, Srinagar, is a heritage hotel offering a **royal experience** amidst the scenic beauty of Kashmir. Originally built in **1910** by Maharaja Pratap Singh, this **luxury hotel** overlooks **Dal Lake** and is surrounded by lush gardens. Guests can indulge in **world-class hospitality**, dine at exquisite restaurants, or relax at the **luxurious spa** while enjoying breathtaking views of the **Himalayas**.

Hotel Features & Amenities

  • Luxury Heritage Hotel
  • Overlooks Dal Lake
  • Spacious & Elegant Rooms
  • Fine Dining Restaurants
  • Spa & Wellness Center
  • Beautiful Garden Surroundings
  • 24/7 Room Service
  • Free Wi-Fi & Parking
